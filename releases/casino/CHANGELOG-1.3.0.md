# SPT Casino 1.3.0

A fifth table: **Horse Racing**. Eight runners over three distances, and the same horses
at all three.

## Horse Racing

Every horse has a speed rating and a stamina rating, and each course weights them
differently. A five-furlong dash is decided by speed and a two-mile marathon by stamina,
so the card turns over as the trip gets longer rather than being the same race at a
different length.

LABS LIGHTNING wins about one race in six over five furlongs, and one in forty over two
miles. SCAV LUCK is its mirror image. DOLLAR SIGN is good everywhere and favourite at
the mile. LEFT BEHIND is bad everywhere, which a card needs -- the longest prices on
every board involve it.

| Course | | Favourite | Longest price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **THE DASH** | 5 furlongs | GRAY GHOST | 1,259 to one |
| **THE MILE** | 8 furlongs | DOLLAR SIGN | 611 to one |
| **THE MARATHON** | 2 miles | NIGHT RAIDER | 758 to one |

Five bets: **WIN**, **PLACE**, **SHOW**, **EXACTA** and **QUINELLA**. A slip takes up to
108 of them at once and they are settled independently, so backing a horse to win *and*
to place pays both when it comes in.

**The whole slip is one transaction.** A bet the window will not accept refuses the slip
rather than charging you for the rest of it.

**6.00% to the house at every spot of every course**, computed from the card rather than
measured by running it. Backing the favourite and backing the outsider are the same bet
in expectation -- the price already contains everything the form tells you. What the form
buys you is a choice about variance, and an actual edge on the exactas, where you are
picking two horses in order and knowing which of them stays is worth something.

Stake roubles, dollars or euros. The dash carries a lower slip ceiling than the other two
courses because it has the longest price on any board; that is arithmetic rather than the
house being careful.
