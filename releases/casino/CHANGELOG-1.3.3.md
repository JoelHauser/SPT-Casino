# SPT Casino 1.3.3

A sixth table: **Casino War**.

## Casino War

One card for you, one for the dealer. High card wins, aces high, even money. On a tie:

- **Go to war:** match your bet. Three cards are burned and one more is dealt to each of
  you. Equal or higher wins.
- **Surrender:** take back half your bet.

An optional **tie bet** pays 10 to 1 if the first two cards tie.

House edge: 2.88% if you always go to war, 3.70% if you always surrender, 18.65% on the tie
bet. Six decks. Bets of 1,000 to 500,000 roubles, or 10 to 5,000 dollars or euros. F12 can
lift the maximum.

**AUTO** keeps dealing until you press STOP, and goes to war on every tie. **SPEED** runs
the table at 1X, 2X, 4X or 6X, like the slot machine.

A tie waits for you if you close the table. The table has its own STATS.

## Also

The lobby tiles light up when you hover over them.

## Installing

Extract `SPT_CasinoV1.3.3.zip` into your SPT folder and overwrite. No change to the other
tables.
