# Blackjack 1.0.1

Appearance only. No rule changes, nothing to do with money, and no reason to
update in a hurry — the table plays identically to 1.0.

## The controls have a face

The buttons were flat rectangles, which is what made the row under the table read
as a dialog box parked beneath a photograph. Nothing in the world is one flat
colour: a physical key catches light along its top edge and loses it underneath.

They are drawn that way now — a vertical gradient with a lit top and a dark
bottom. Hovering lifts the face and turns the edge gold; pressing darkens and
flattens it, so it reads as the button going *in* rather than merely changing
colour. Labels are letter-spaced, the way a label is engraved onto a control
rather than printed near it.

**DEAL is brass**, matching the fittings on the table, and it is the only one —
it is the only button that starts a round, where the rest are choices inside a
hand already running. The selected currency chip is brass too.

**The wager field runs its gradient the other way**, so it reads as a slot cut
into the bar while the buttons sit proud of it.

## The betting bar fits its contents

It was 1340 wide holding rows of 832 and 852, carrying more than 400 of empty box
on either side of the controls. That is what made it look like a panel the
buttons had been dropped into rather than one built around them.

It is 880 now, which is the widest row plus its own padding, and the rows beneath
came in to match so the whole block reads as one column.

## DEAL says when it cannot deal

If the bet is larger than you are carrying, DEAL greys rather than looking ready
and then refusing. It stays clickable — the refusal explains itself, and a button
that silently does nothing is worse than one that answers.

## Installing over 1.0

Extract over the top; nothing has moved. Your statistics and your BepInEx
settings are untouched, since neither the mod's folder layout nor its GUID has
changed.
